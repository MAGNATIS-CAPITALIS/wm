### What you need to file it in California:

*1. File Online:* Go to http://bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov - File LLC-1 - Fee is $70
*2. Name Requirements:* Your name must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company" and must be distinguishable. Search it first on the SOS Business Search. 
You cannot use "Bank," "Trust," or "Insurance" in the name.
*3. Registered Agent:* Must be a California resident or a company authorized to be an agent in CA with a physical address.
*4. After Filing - Required:*
   - *Form LLC-12 (Statement of Information):* Due within 90 days of formation, then every 2 years. $20 fee.
   - *Operating Agreement:* California requires you to HAVE one, even though you don't file it. I can draft this for you next - it should cover ownership %, capital contributions, fiduciary standards, and what happens if a partner leaves.
   - *EIN:* Get free from http://IRS.gov
   - *RIA Registration:* For wealth management, you will need to register as an Investment Adviser with the DFPI or SEC. An LLC alone does not let you give investment advice for compensation.
